## Key Learnings

- Ownership should be assigned to the team responsible for solving the root cause rather than the team that first notices the issue.
- Effective task routing ensures work reaches the right people in the correct order.
- Large workplace problems require collaboration between multiple departments instead of relying on a single individual.
- Product Managers coordinate priorities while Engineering teams implement solutions and QA verifies quality.
- Customer Support provides valuable feedback that helps Product and Engineering improve user experience.
- Cross-functional communication is essential for delivering reliable products on time.

---

## Reflection

This simulation improved my understanding of how responsibilities are distributed across different teams in a technology company. I learned that successful organizations rely on clear ownership, structured workflows, and effective collaboration to solve complex business problems. Instead of assigning every issue to one person, different departments contribute according to their expertise, resulting in better decision-making and faster problem resolution.
