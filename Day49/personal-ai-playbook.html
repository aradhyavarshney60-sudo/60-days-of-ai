What this tool does

This is your personal library of reusable AI prompt systems for research, writing, studying, and coding. My Workflows holds ready-to-use templates you can copy into Claude or ChatGPT. Prompt Builder lets you assemble a custom prompt from labeled building blocks (role, objective, format, etc). Loop Builder turns any prompt into a self-checking, self-improving instruction set for the AI to iterate on. Nothing here leaves your browser — everything saves to this device only.

The short version

This page is a self-contained library of AI prompt templates ("workflows") plus two tools for building your own prompts. Everything is saved in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere.

Dashboard

An overview: quick stats, your workflow categories, and recently used templates.

My Workflows

Your saved prompt templates. Each card shows the category, a description, and its editable variables. Use the toolbar to search, filter, favorite, duplicate, or edit.

Categories

Workflows are grouped by what they're for: Research & Reading (literature review, summarizing papers), Writing & Essays, Study & Exam Prep, and Coding & Data.

Prompt Builder

Assemble a prompt piece by piece from building blocks like Role, Objective, Context, Constraints, Reasoning Strategy, Output Format, Tone, Examples, and Quality Checks. Each block explains what it does. Watch the live preview update as you add blocks, then save it as a new workflow.

Loop Builder

Takes any prompt and wraps it in an autonomous loop structure — the AI is told to check its own work against criteria you set, keep improving, and stop only when your stop conditions are met. Useful for iterative drafts, code refinement, or study-guide polishing.
